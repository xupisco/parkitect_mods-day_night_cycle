[h1]Day Night Cycle[/h1]
The game can switch between day and night, but the sun stays in the same place. With this mod the sun crosses the sky, the shadows follow it, and the park goes through sunrise, sunset and a gradual night.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]The sun rises, crosses the sky and sets, and the shadows move with it
[*]The light and the sky turn warm near sunrise and sunset
[*]The light fades into a dark night with a blue moonlight
[*]The park's lights turn on at dusk and off at dawn
[*]The clock follows the park's time, so it pauses and speeds up with the game
[/list]

[h2]How to use it[/h2]
[list]
[*]Open a park and the cycle is already running. Press [b]H[/b] to open the window. The key can be changed in the game's controls settings.
[*][b]Day and night cycle[/b] turns the mod on or off.
[*][b]Stop the clock[/b] holds the current hour.
[*][b]Time of day[/b] takes you to any hour.
[*][b]Day length[/b] sets how long a full day takes, from 1 to 24 of the game's weeks. The default is 6, the same as the game's own automatic cycle.
[*][b]Sunset color[/b] sets how warm the sunrise and sunset look.
[/list]
Your values are saved when you close the window, and they apply to all your parks.

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]While the cycle is on, it decides when it's night, so the game's own day and night button has no effect. Turn the cycle off to get the button back.
[*]Rain still dims the sun and removes the shadows, and a builder window still brightens the night so you can see what you're building.
[*]Night only changes the look of the park and turns on its lights.
[*]It can't be used in multiplayer yet, because it hasn't been tested with two players.
[/list]

Your saves don't depend on this mod. If you remove it, the game's light goes back to normal.
